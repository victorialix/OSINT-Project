# Data Analysis Summary

The application collects real-time data from four APIs: city lookup, weather forecast, travel advisory, and currency exchange. The weather data is analyzed to compute the 7‑day average temperature, which is used to determine comfort level and packing recommendations. Advisory data is analyzed to determine the safety level of the destination. Currency data is analyzed to understand the strength of the local currency relative to USD.

These three factors are combined into a travel intelligence score. Higher scores indicate safer destinations with favorable weather and currency conditions. The analysis shows that destinations with low advisory risk and moderate temperatures tend to produce the highest travel scores. The generated chart visually represents temperature trends, helping users understand climate patterns before traveling.
